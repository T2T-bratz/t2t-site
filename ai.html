How AI is used on this site

Every article here carries a label saying how it was made. I use AI. I'm not going to pretend otherwise, and I'm not going to make you guess. The label tells you how much of the thinking was mine.

Over-amplified profiles and content that hides how it was made are the two things I like least about this space. This page is the fix for the second one.

Written by Brad

No AI involved at any stage.

Written by Brad, edited with AI

I wrote it. AI tightened the prose, cut repetition, or caught errors. Every idea and every sentence's intent is mine.

Drafted by AI, reviewed by Brad

AI produced the draft from my outline or notes. I reviewed and edited the whole piece and stand behind the argument. The point of view is mine; some of the words are not.

Drafted by AI, facts checked by a human

AI produced the draft and most of the prose. A human verified every factual claim, source, statistic and link: that each source exists, is cited correctly, and actually says what the article says it says. The prose was not rewritten line by line.
